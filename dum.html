AI Literacy Advocacy

Artificial Intelligence is becoming an important part of modern society. From recommendation systems to medical analysis, AI technologies are increasingly influencing how people work, learn, and make decisions.

Artificial Intelligence shaping modern society

Our Advocacy

The purpose of T-Know is to advocate for a deeper understanding of how Artificial Intelligence works.

Many people use AI tools every day, but few understand their underlying processes, capabilities, and limitations.

By promoting AI literacy, we aim to help communities make informed decisions about technology and its role in society.

Why Understanding AI Matters

  • To understand what AI can do and what it cannot do.
  • To prevent misinformation and unrealistic expectations about AI.
  • To help individuals use AI responsibly and ethically.
  • To empower communities with knowledge about emerging technologies.

Understanding technology empowers society

Empowering Communities

When people understand the strengths and weaknesses of AI, they become empowered to use technology wisely.

Knowledge about AI allows individuals to:

  • Use AI tools for education and productivity.
  • Recognize biases or errors in AI-generated results.
  • Participate in discussions about technology policy and ethics.

Our Vision

Our vision is a society where people are not just users of artificial intelligence but also informed thinkers who understand how these systems operate.

Through education and awareness, AI can become a tool that supports learning, innovation, and the overall well-being of communities.
