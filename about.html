About Us

We are a group of students who created this website as part of a school project. Our goal was to make a resource that helps other students understand the complex topic of Machine Learning in a simple and approachable way. We wanted to combine our interests in technology and education to produce a website that is both informative and easy to navigate. By presenting clear explanations, historical context, key figures, and practical applications, we aim to make learning Machine Learning less intimidating for beginners. This project also allowed us to practice research, web development, and communication skills while contributing something useful to our peers. Ultimately, our hope is that other students will find this website helpful for studying, projects, or general curiosity about Machine Learning.

Even though we are students ourselves, we believe that creating educational tools can make a meaningful impact, encouraging others to explore, learn, and engage with technology in a productive and enjoyable way. This website is purely educational, meant to serve as a reference for students, and demonstrates that learning can be fun and collaborative when approached creatively.

Our brand name, T-Know, represents the idea of “To Know” — emphasizing the pursuit of knowledge, especially in technology and Machine Learning. The “T” also highlights technology, making it a simple but meaningful reminder to always seek more tech knowledge and understanding. We chose this name because it reflects the purpose of our website: to provide students with accessible information so they can “know more” and become confident in exploring tech topics.

As for our logo, it features a circular design with a modern, tech-inspired aesthetic. The circle represents unity and continuous learning, while the central icon symbolizes knowledge, curiosity, and exploration in the field of technology. It is designed to be memorable, approachable, and visually communicates our mission: to educate and inspire students to learn more about Machine Learning and technology in general.
